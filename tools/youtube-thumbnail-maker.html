How to Make a YouTube Thumbnail

  1. Choose a template or change the background color.
  2. Upload your photo and move it into position.
  3. Add text, adjust its font, color, outline and shadow.
  4. Download your thumbnail as PNG or JPG.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended thumbnail size?

Use a 1280 × 720 pixel image with a 16:9 aspect ratio.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes images in your browser.

Can I download thumbnails for free?

Yes. You can download your design as PNG or JPG without an account.

Can I use my thumbnail commercially?

Use images and assets you own or are licensed to use. Create original designs rather than copying another creator's exact thumbnail.
