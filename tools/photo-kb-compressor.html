How to compress a photo

  1. Select a photo from your device.
  2. Enter the desired size and choose KB or MB.
  3. Click Compress Photo and wait for the result.
  4. Check the final size, then download your image.

Note: Exact file sizes cannot be guaranteed for every photo. If the original image is already smaller than your target, this tool will not enlarge it unnecessarily. Compression converts the output to JPEG, so transparent backgrounds will not be preserved.
